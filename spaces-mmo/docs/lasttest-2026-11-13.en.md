# Load test 2026-11-13: 200 players who play everything, big and locally under a profiler

`tests/perf/play-load.ts` ([betrieb.en.md](betrieb.en.md), "Players who play everything"): 200 registered players, epoch 4
forced, activities of all four epochs at random with 1 to 3 s between two commands; model calls off while they played.
Each player's things come from the driver (into the pack through the region spaces), so the world fills faster than with
real players: a run of 30 minutes took the regions from 2,600–7,000 things to 15,000–40,000.

## big, 30 minutes (commit 59a52a1)

Backed up before (`mmo-backup` by hand, big and nano) and put back afterwards (the whole game stopped, the four world
Spaces, the NPC memory and nano's accounts Space written back, counts checked against the ones before). The run of 300
players was not started.

Twenty minutes after the end the Space processes still ran at 75–100 %, with no player left: the world kept the things
the run had made, and every commit costs more with them (below).

## Locally, the web process under a profiler

The local test bed (`docker:local:rust`) with its web container stopped and `mmo-web` built with line tables
(`CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_DEBUG=line-tables-only`) on this machine; `eu-stack -i` sampled it about once a second (no perf
here), 15 minutes, 200 players, the test bed's volumes put back before each run and after.

Before: busy in 94 % of the samples. 72 % in `atlas_state`: on every change of the overview it ran for every client and
went over every object of the world twice (`stops`, the same for every client, and `todos_for`), and it read three
settings from the space each time (600 calls per broadcast at 200 players). 9 % in `msg_view` → `for_viewer`: a Unicode
regex of the viewer's name compiled anew for every message and every viewer.

The fix: `atlas_shared` builds what every client's state shares once per broadcast (the owned things, the objects each
character has tasks of, people, fog, fires, epochs, stops) and reads the settings once; `named_in` keeps the compiled
name pattern per name. The frames are the same (`web-cross`: no differences).

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `mmo-web` busy | 94 % | 28 % |
| Commands in 900 s | 19,792 | 29,739 |
| Median in the first 2 minutes | 5.0 s | 0.6 s |
| Unanswered after 10 s, whole run | 33 % | 11 % |

After the fix the answers still slow down as the world fills (median 8 s after 15 minutes): the Space processes are the
limit now and stay busy after the players are gone. A few gdb stacks of one (ost) show commits, a lease renewal among
them, settling waiting operations (`Space::settle_batch` → `serve` → `find` → `Template::matches`), a scan of the
entries per waiter. Not yet profiled further.

Raw data: big `~/phi-load/2026-11-13/` (`bots-200/`), this machine `~/phi-load/2026-11-13/profile*`.
