# Requirements: website of the Lindenhof joinery

The Lindenhof joinery (12 employees, one workshop, Upper Austria) replaces its ten-year-old website. This list is
all there is; nothing has been built or bought yet.

## Who it is for

- R1. Private customers who want furniture made to measure (kitchens, wardrobes, tables).
- R2. Architects and builders who look for a partner for interior fittings.
- R3. Applicants: the joinery takes two apprentices a year.

## Content

- R4. A page per kind of work (kitchens, living, office, restoration) with photos and a short text.
- R5. A gallery of finished projects: 40 at the start, about 15 more a year; each with 5 to 12 photos, the year,
  the materials and a text of up to 200 words. Projects can be filtered by kind of work and by wood.
- R6. A page about the workshop and the people, with the opening hours and a map.
- R7. A page for apprenticeships with an application form (name, e-mail, phone, a letter, a PDF of up to 5 MB).
- R8. A contact form for enquiries: name, e-mail, phone, kind of work, a text, up to three photos or plans.
- R9. German and English; German is written first, English may follow days later, and a page without an English
  version shows the German one with a notice.

## Editing

- R10. Two employees without technical training maintain all content, photos included, in a browser. They upload
  photos as they come from a phone (up to 12 MB each); the site produces the sizes it needs.
- R11. A change is visible to the public at most five minutes after it is saved. A draft can be previewed first.
- R12. Earlier versions of a page can be brought back for 90 days.

## Quality

- R13. Pages load fast on a phone on a weak connection: the largest element visible within 2.5 seconds on a 4G
  connection, a gallery page under 1.5 MB on first load.
- R14. Accessible to WCAG 2.2 level AA.
- R15. Found by search engines: own title and description per page, structured data for the business, a sitemap.
- R16. The old site's 60 addresses keep working (redirects).

## Law and data

- R17. Hosting in the EU. No cookies that need consent; statistics without personal data.
- R18. Form submissions go by e-mail to the office and are stored for 6 months, then deleted. Applications are
  visible to the two owners only.
- R19. Imprint and privacy notice as Austrian law requires; a spam protection for the forms that needs no third
  party outside the EU.

## Operation and money

- R20. No employee can run a server. Operation must need no more than one hour a month of an outside person.
- R21. Running cost at most 60 euros a month, everything included. The budget for building it is 18 000 euros.
- R22. Daily backups, restorable within one working day; the joinery can leave the provider with all content and
  photos in a usable form.
- R23. Online within 10 weeks of the start; the gallery may open with 15 projects.

## Not wanted

- R24. No shop, no prices, no customer login, no newsletter, no chat.
